# How to Choose a Branding Agency in Bangalore: 10 Questions Every Founder Should Ask

Bangalore has no shortage of creative agencies, from solo designers to large networks. That's good for choice and bad for clarity. Every agency shows polished case studies and promises "brand transformation." So how do you separate a real partner from a pretty portfolio?

This guide gives you a practical process: what to look for, 10 questions to ask, red flags to avoid and a simple scorecard to compare agencies side by side.

## First, know what you need

Before you contact anyone, write a one-page brief that answers:

- What do we sell, and to whom?
- What's not working today (low trust, low sales, inconsistent look)?
- What do we need: strategy, identity, packaging, website, video, marketplace work or all of these?
- What's the timeline and approximate budget?
- Who makes the final decision on our side?

A clear brief gets you better proposals and shows agencies you're serious.

## 10 questions to ask a branding agency

### 1. "Can you show work for brands like mine?"
Look for relevance in stage, industry or business model, not just famous names. A food-and-beverage founder should see food-and-beverage work. A D2C founder should see packaging and marketplace work.

### 2. "What is your process from brief to launch?"
A good answer includes research, positioning, concept development, feedback rounds, final delivery and handover. Vague answers like "we'll make something creative" are a warning.

### 3. "Who will actually work on my project?"
Meet the people doing the work, not only the salesperson. Ask about the strategist, designer, developer and project manager.

### 4. "How do you handle strategy before design?"
The best agencies ask uncomfortable questions about your customer and competitors before drawing a logo.

### 5. "What exactly is included, and what costs extra?"
Get deliverables in writing: number of concepts, revision rounds, file formats, guidelines, templates, ownership of source files.

### 6. "How do you measure whether branding worked?"
Branding isn't only about looking good. Ask how they connect the work to outcomes such as better conversion, stronger recall, higher-quality enquiries or improved marketplace performance.

### 7. "Can you support us after launch?"
Branding is useful only when it's applied. Check if the agency can help with website, social media, video, packaging production and marketplace listings so the brand stays consistent.

### 8. "How do you work with feedback?"
You want a structured process: a single point of contact, clear timelines and consolidated feedback, not endless back-and-forth.

### 9. "Can we speak to past clients?"
Real clients will talk. Ask about communication, deadlines, how problems were handled and whether results matched expectations.

### 10. "What would you not recommend for us right now?"
A confident agency will tell you what to skip. If every answer is "all of it," be cautious.

## Red flags to watch for

- Guaranteed outcomes ("we'll make you go viral")
- No discussion of your customers or competitors
- Unusually low pricing with a long list of "free" extras
- Portfolio without explanation of the problem and result
- Pressure to sign quickly
- No clear ownership terms for final files
- One person doing everything with no backup

## Local vs remote: does a Bangalore agency matter?

Many branding projects run fully online, so location is not always decisive. But a Bangalore-based agency can help when you:

- Want in-person workshops or product and packaging reviews
- Need local video shoots or photography
- Are building for a South Indian or Karnataka audience and want cultural context
- Prefer quick meetings with the team

If your market is national or global, focus on capability and fit first and geography second.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I shortlist branding agencies?
Pick three to five based on portfolio relevance and reviews, send the same brief to each, and compare proposals using a scorecard.

### Should I choose a big agency or a smaller one?
Choose based on access, specialisation and fit. Smaller teams often give founders more direct attention, while larger ones offer breadth. Ask who will actually work on your project.

### How long does a branding project usually take?
Timelines depend on scope. Identity work usually takes weeks, while packaging, website and video add more time. Ask each agency for a milestone plan.

### Can one agency handle branding, website and marketing?
Yes. A full-service agency keeps strategy and execution aligned, which is helpful for consistency and speed.

### What should I prepare before meeting an agency?
Your one-page brief, competitor examples you like and dislike, target customer details, budget range and timeline. See our [FAQ](/faq.html) for more on working with us.
