SEO for Startups in India: A Practical 90-Day Plan for Founders

By Branding Bots Team · 27 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

Paid ads stop the moment you stop paying. SEO (search engine optimisation) builds an asset that keeps bringing in customers, often at a lower cost over time. For a startup with a limited budget, ranking for the right searches can become one of the most reliable growth channels.

The catch: SEO is a compounding channel, not an overnight one. Here's a practical 90-day plan to build a strong foundation.

What SEO actually is

SEO is the process of helping search engines understand your website and showing it to people searching for what you offer. It rests on four pillars:

  1. Technical: can Google crawl and load your site quickly?
  2. On-page: does each page clearly target a topic or keyword?
  3. Content: do you answer searchers' questions better than others?
  4. Authority: do other reputable sites mention and link to you?

Before you start: set realistic expectations

  • Results typically build over months, not days. Competitive keywords take longer.
  • You can't guarantee a specific ranking. Anyone who does is overpromising.
  • SEO works best alongside a strong brand, a fast website and clear conversion paths.

Days 1 to 30: Foundation

Week 1: Set up tracking and fix the basics

  • Install Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console
  • Submit your XML sitemap
  • Make sure the site uses HTTPS and has no broken pages
  • Check mobile usability and loading speed (Google PageSpeed Insights)

Week 2: Keyword research

Make a list of what your customers search for. Start with:

  • Service or product keywords: "branding agency in Bangalore," "organic skincare online"
  • Problem keywords: "how to increase Amazon sales," "how to price a product"
  • Comparison keywords: "agency vs freelancer," "Shopify vs WooCommerce"
  • Local keywords: your city plus service

Group keywords by search intent:

IntentWhat the searcher wantsBest page type
InformationalTo learnBlog post, guide
CommercialTo compare optionsComparison, "best of," service page
TransactionalTo buy or hireProduct or service page
NavigationalTo find a brandHomepage, contact page

Free tools like Google Search Console, Google Keyword Planner, Google autocomplete and "People also ask" are enough to start.

Week 3: Map keywords to pages

Give each important keyword a single page. Avoid targeting the same keyword on multiple pages, which makes them compete with each other.

Week 4: Optimise key pages

For homepage, service and product pages:

  • One clear H1 with the primary keyword
  • Title tag (about 50 to 60 characters) and meta description (about 150 to 155 characters)
  • Descriptive, short URLs
  • Helpful headings (H2, H3)
  • Image alt text
  • Internal links to related pages
  • A clear call to action

Our startup website checklist covers the design and technical side in more detail.

Days 31 to 60: Content

Build a content plan around customer questions

Aim for a mix:

  • Pillar guides on core topics (long, complete)
  • How-to articles answering specific questions
  • Comparison and decision posts for buyers close to purchase
  • Case studies showing real results

How to write content that ranks

  1. Search your target keyword and study the top results
  2. Identify what they cover and what they miss
  3. Create a clearer, more useful, more current page
  4. Use simple language, short paragraphs and descriptive headings
  5. Add FAQs, examples, images or tables
  6. Show real experience and expertise. Google rewards content that demonstrates first-hand knowledge and trustworthiness.

Publish consistently

A realistic plan is 4 to 8 quality articles in these 30 days. Quality beats volume. One strong guide can outperform ten thin posts.

Internal linking

Link new posts to relevant service pages and to each other. This helps search engines understand your site and guides readers toward conversion.

Days 61 to 90: Authority and local presence

Earn quality backlinks

Links from relevant, reputable sites signal trust. Ways to earn them:

  • Publish original insights or data from your own work
  • Contribute guest articles to industry blogs
  • Get listed in genuine directories and startup communities
  • Share useful tools, templates or checklists others will cite
  • Partner with complementary businesses for features

Avoid buying links or joining link schemes. They can lead to penalties.

Strengthen local SEO

If you serve a specific city or region:

  • Create and complete your Google Business Profile
  • Keep name, address and phone number identical everywhere
  • Collect genuine customer reviews
  • Add city-specific content and service pages
  • Embed a map and add local schema where relevant

Improve conversion

Traffic only matters if it turns into enquiries or sales. Add clear calls to action, simple forms, WhatsApp contact, testimonials and case studies on high-traffic pages.

Technical SEO essentials

  • Core Web Vitals: aim for fast loading, stable layout and responsive interaction
  • Mobile-first indexing: Google primarily uses the mobile version of your site
  • Schema markup: FAQ, Organization, Local Business, Article and Product schema help search engines understand your content
  • Canonical tags: prevent duplicate content issues
  • Clean site structure: every important page within a few clicks of the homepage
  • Robots.txt and sitemap: make sure you aren't blocking important pages

How to measure SEO progress

Review monthly:

  • Impressions and clicks in Search Console
  • Rankings for your target keywords
  • Organic traffic in Google Analytics
  • Conversions from organic visitors (forms, calls, sales)
  • Pages indexed and any coverage errors
  • Backlinks gained

Expect early wins from long-tail and local keywords first, then gradual growth for more competitive terms.

Common startup SEO mistakes

  • Writing for search engines instead of people
  • Ignoring search intent
  • Targeting only very competitive keywords
  • Publishing thin, copied or purely AI-generated content with no real expertise or editing
  • Neglecting site speed and mobile experience
  • Not tracking conversions
  • Giving up after two months

SEO and branding work together

Strong brands get more branded searches, more clicks and more trust, all of which support SEO. A fast, well-structured website and consistent content make it easier to rank and convert. Our digital marketing and SEO services and SEO-friendly website design are built to work together.

Frequently asked questions

How long does SEO take to show results?

Many sites see early movement within a few months, but meaningful growth often takes longer depending on competition, content quality and site authority.

Can a new website rank on Google?

Yes. Start with specific, lower-competition keywords and build topical depth and authority over time.

How many blog posts do I need?

There's no magic number. Focus on covering your core topics thoroughly and updating content regularly.

Is SEO better than paid ads?

They serve different purposes. Ads give faster results but stop when spend stops. SEO takes longer but compounds. Many startups use both.

Do I need to hire an SEO agency?

Not always at the start. You can handle basics in-house, then bring in specialists when you need technical audits, content scale or competitive keywords.
